## Final prompt

Use case: style-transfer.
Asset: wide watercolor illustration for Leximory's native iPhone and iPad membership sheet.
Image 1 is the earlier concept to edit: an open book becomes a little green world, with a white and a black cat. Keep that central idea and gently elevated three-quarter view.
Images 2 and 3 are the authoritative Leximory identity and painting references. Redraw the entire concept in THEIR simple pencil and watercolor language. The two cats must be the same rounded, flat, slightly naive white and charcoal cats as the Leximory icon: small grey dot eyes, tiny inverted-Y nose, irregular pencil outlines, very simple bodies. No realistic fur, no realistic kitten anatomy or glossy eyes.
Simplify the book-world drastically: a single pale sage lawn grows across the two open cream pages, a narrow blank path follows the fold into a small rolling hill. White cat sits on left page looking at charcoal cat resting on right page. A few sparse wispy grass strokes, one small lime bookmark ribbon. Book has softly sketched paper edges and muted sage cover.
Remove the village, buildings, mountains, river, bridge, flowers, tall branches and golden sun from image 1. No text, no letters, no decorative sparkles, no vignette, no halo. Use white, cream, pale sage #DDE7C7, muted grey-green #9CAEA1, charcoal. Delicate translucent watercolor and imperfect graphite on paper. Wide low composition, about 2:1, with comfortable breathing room around all edges. Only the isolated book, cats and little lawn; genuinely transparent background beyond the irregular painted silhouette. Readable at 350 points wide.
