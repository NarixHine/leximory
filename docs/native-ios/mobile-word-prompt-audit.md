# Mobile word prompt source review

Reviewed on 2026-10-19. The five active mobile guides contain 20 demonstration outputs. This record covers their lexical facts and presentation. It does not measure model hallucination rates.

## Presentation correction

The first mobile drafts changed the established annotation style and used elementary examples. The current guides preserve the language-specific presentation while teaching contextual distinctions in literary and expository passages.

- Japanese uses `**［词性］（读音／中文义）** 日文解释`, with an optional kanji spelling in the parentheses for kana selections. It does not append a separate invented sentence and Chinese translation. Four of its five shots adapt vocabulary and contexts from the existing article shots. The additional idiom demonstrates preserving a lexicalized negative and distinguishing understanding an argument from accepting it.
- Ruby is conditional on difficult or unfamiliar kanji. Both ruby and ordinary Japanese explanations appear in the shots.
- French retains the bold Chinese gloss, IPA, French explanation, and optional etymology/cognate fields. The shots now concern ratifying an entrenched imbalance, invoking a title for preferential treatment, and an apparently available freedom that cannot be exercised.
- Classical Chinese retains the compact three-field dictionary style and contexts drawn from the existing article prompt. Its shots demonstrate an institutional counting unit, a historical diplomatic term, a contextual temporal sense, and an uncommon reading.
